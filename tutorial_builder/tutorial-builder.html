Tutorial Builder

Turn a screen recording into a step-by-step tutorial

Open a recording with your narration. The builder writes out what you said, splits it into steps where the screen changes, and takes a picture for each step. Then you edit it and export a tutorial page.

Everything happens in this browser. Your recording is not uploaded anywhere. The speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and kept for next time.

Have a project file from before?

Already have captions for this recording? before opening the video and the builder will use them instead of transcribing.
